# datastory_5
[Link to video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HF4nlI_DsjU)

I have created a data entry app for my forestry lab group to help collect the data of out sites for our stand. This project is focused on making a future management plan for compartment 60. This compartment has a rare species of hill cane call Arundinaria Appilachiana. This hill cane was discovered on campus in 2019 and the university has continued to manage and study it. The mystery of the plant is that the university has never witnessed it bloom. This means it hasn’t been reproducing. So, this plan is an experiment with the forest conditions to see what is right for this plant to bloom.
